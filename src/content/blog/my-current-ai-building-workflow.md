I keep switching tools. The space moves fast and what works best changes every few months. This is a running log of what I'm using, what I've tried, and what I'm watching. Latest on top.

---

### Mar 2026 — now

**Main tools:** Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity

- **Codex** — deep implementation, finding bugs, clean UI work. Smooth worktree integration for juggling multiple tasks. Also using it for PR reviews.
- **Claude Code** — terminal-first coding sessions.
- **Antigravity** — manual reading and editing files when I want more control.
- **OpenClaw** — runs on a Raspberry Pi, used through WhatsApp for automation and edits on the go. Things like migrating a Next.js app or spinning up a quick app for a specific need.

---

### Jan 2026 — Mar 2026

**Main tools:** Antigravity

- **Antigravity** — Plan Mode and PR workflow. Felt like the most complete integration at the time — planning, implementing, and reviewing all in one place.

Started noticing Codex was better for harder, more complex tasks though.

---

### Oct 2025 — Jan 2026

**Main tools:** Cline (VS Code), Gemini, ChatGPT

- **Cline (VS Code)** — Plan-then-Act mode. Used Grok through Cline because it was free and fast.
- **Gemini** — used for PRDs.
- **ChatGPT** — general purpose, quick questions.

By the end of this period I wanted something more integrated. That's what pulled me toward Antigravity.

---

### Also tried at some point

**Amp**, **OpenCode** — haven't spent enough time with either yet, but their architecture looks interesting. Want to go deeper.

### Watching

**Pi**, **Minions (Stripe)** — curious about both, haven't gone deep yet.
